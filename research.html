Current Academic Focus

University of Vermont Doctoral Research

I am currently conducting academic research under the supervision of Professor Luis Duffaut Espinosa at the University of Vermont. My primary focus lies in bridging complex mathematical theory with physical hardware implementations.

A major cornerstone of my recent work involves designing a model-free formation control framework for decoupled multi-agent systems, featuring active disturbance estimation. I recently authored the research paper "Decoupled Robust State Estimation and Disturbance Rejection in Model-Free Formation Control", detailing architectures designed to handle uncertainty in multi-agent robotics.
